# Code of conduct

We want our repositories to be a friendly, respectful place for everyone, regardless of experience, background or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient; assume good intent.
- Keep discussions on topic and constructive: critique code and ideas, not people.
- Respect other people's time, privacy and differing opinions.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, personal or political attacks
- Sexualized language or imagery
- Publishing anyone's private information, including data from OneTrace.pro projects, without explicit permission
- Spam, trolling and any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors who do so.

Report problems to [support@onetrace.pro](mailto:support@onetrace.pro) with the subject **Code of conduct**. Reports are handled confidentially.

This code of conduct is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
